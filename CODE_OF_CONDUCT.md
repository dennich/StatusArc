# Community Guidelines

StatusArc aims to be a practical, welcoming open-source project.

When participating:

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give technical criticism specifically and respectfully.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, or deliberately expose another person's private
  information.
- Do not post credentials, private network details, or other secrets.
- Assume mistakes can be corrected and focus on making the project better.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that undermine a safe and
productive technical community, and may restrict participation when necessary.
